FAQ

Common Questions

Have a question not covered here? Reach out directly — we're always happy to talk through your project.

Call Us Directly
How quickly can I get a quote?
We typically respond within 24 hours of your initial contact. In many cases, we can schedule a walkthrough the same week. Call or text us at (585) 415-4023 or (585) 363-0930 for the fastest response.
Do you handle drywall repairs before painting?
Absolutely. We offer complete drywall repair, patching, and skim coat services as part of a full paint package or as a standalone service. Proper prep is what ensures a premium paint finish — we don't skip it.
Do you work on commercial properties?
Yes. We handle office spaces, retail locations, restaurants, warehouses, and multi-unit properties. We can schedule work during off-hours or weekends to minimize disruption to your business operations.
Do you serve areas outside Rochester?
Yes. We serve Gates, Pittsford, Irondequoit, Henrietta, Webster, Greece, Brighton, and beyond Rochester — including Syracuse and Buffalo for commercial projects. Contact us to discuss your location.
Do you offer flooring installation?
Yes — hardwood, luxury vinyl plank (LVP), laminate, tile, and more. We handle everything from subfloor prep to final transitions and trim work for a complete, seamless installation.
Can I reach you in Spanish?
Yes. We offer full bilingual service in English and Spanish — from the initial estimate through the final walkthrough. We serve our entire community with clear, comfortable communication. / Sí, ofrecemos servicio completo en español.
How do I prepare for a paint job?
Very little preparation is needed from you. We move and cover furniture, protect flooring, and tape all trim. For best results, clear countertops and remove small décor items before we arrive. We'll walk you through everything at the estimate.
